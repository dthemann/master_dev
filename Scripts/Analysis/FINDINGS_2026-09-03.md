# Adversarial validation of Tables 17, 18 and 19 against the Results, Discussion and Conclusions

**Date:** 2026-09-03
**Trigger:** validate that the Results, Discussion and Conclusions match the extended
Tables 18 and 19, and that all prose referencing them was adequately updated.
**Outcome:** one pre-existing critical defect found and fixed, five staleness defects
introduced by the 2026-09-02 table extension found and fixed. 459/459 assertions
reproduce, the short build is 142 pages with zero undefined references.

---

## A. The critical finding: Table 21 was never reproducible from the pipeline

Appendix C declares, at the head of the recovery chapter:

> The family is computed on the gnina-rescored AutoDock Vina arm that the recovery
> tables below display, ranked by that arm's own rescored order.

Table 21 prints that arm's rates on every cross-tool row: 37.3 / 62.0 / 65.0 / 66.3.

`_TOPK_TEST_PAIRS` in `posebusters_pose_comparison.py` used **raw Vina** as the
cross-tool baseline: 31.4 / 50.8 / 58.7 / 61.1. Checked across the whole file history,
the gnina baseline appears in **no commit**. The script therefore tested a contrast the
thesis never reported.

The consequence was not cosmetic. Running the pipeline produced Holm p of 1, 0.776,
0.0668 and 0.0489 against DiffDock (raw), where the thesis prints 0.694, 0.0002, 0.0003
and 0.0005, and turned the thesis's "significant at every deeper pool" into *ns* at
three of the four depths.

**The thesis was right and the script was wrong.** Substituting the declared gnina
baseline and the then-pooled 24-test family reproduces the printed note exactly:

| quantity | thesis note | reconstruction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| vs DiffDock (raw), k = 1 / 5 / 10 / 15 | 0.694, 0.0002, 0.0003, 0.0005 | 0.6938, 0.0001763, 0.0003455, 0.0004829 |
| vs DiffDock (gnina-opt) | 0.709, 0.010, 0.014, 0.047 | 0.7093, 0.009503, 0.01392, 0.04678 |
| rank-1 discordant pairs | 62/51 and 60/55 | 62/51 and 60/55 |

**Why it survived every prior review:** Table 21 was the one appendix float with no
assertion behind it. The harness covered Tables 1, 2, 5, 6, 8, 18 and 19 and reported
378/378 while this table was unreproducible.

**Fixed:** the baseline in `_TOPK_TEST_PAIRS` is now the gnina arm, with the reason in
a comment at the point of change. `check_table_21` in `thesis_assertions.py` now pins
the rates, the discordant counts and the corrected p-values, so the harness fails if
the baseline drifts again. Total checks 378 -> 459.

## B. Adding the two requested rows breaks a cross-tool verdict under pooling

The 2026-09-02 request added AutoDock raw and DiffDock + smina to Tables 18 and 19.
Under the thesis's original single pooled family this is not a safe edit. Extending the
pool from 24 to 32 tests costs an unrelated cross-tool contrast its significance:

| contrast | pooled 24 | pooled 32 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AutoDock + gnina vs DiffDock + gnina, k = 15 | 0.047 (*) | 0.075 (ns) |

A within-tool optimisation row changing a between-tool verdict is not a methodological
result. This is the concrete justification for splitting the family, which was done on
2026-09-02 and is retained. Under two families of sixteen, with the baseline corrected,
**every qualitative claim in Table 21's note survives** and its own phrase "those
sixteen tests" becomes literally true for the first time.

Between-tool family as it now stands, all four cross-tool contrasts on the declared arm:

## D. A tension I first described wrongly, then corrected

The Discussion says "DiffDock + smina led gnina". That is true on the **selection
criterion**, the triple gate at top-15, at 133 complexes to 132 and 834 qualifying poses
to 828. Before the extension, Table 18 did not carry the smina arm, so nothing in the
appendix let a reader check the claim.

My first repair asserted that gnina is "level or marginally ahead" of smina at all 30
depths. **That is false**, and it went into the thesis before I caught it. The lead
crosses over:

| depth band | leader | both endpoints agree |
| --- | --- | --- |
| k = 1 to 6 | gnina | yes |
| k = 7 to 8 | tied | yes |
| k = 9 to 13 | **smina** | yes |
| k = 14 to 18 | tied | yes |
| k = 19 to 30 | gnina | yes |

Of the four depths Table 18 prints, gnina leads at k = 1 and 5, smina leads at k = 10 and
they tie at k = 15. The largest gap at any depth on either endpoint is 0.99 percentage
points.

A second overstatement in the same repair: the two arms recover 167 complexes each under
the double gate, but **not the same 167**. They differ on three complexes either way, so
the sets intersect at 164. Under the triple gate they disagree on nine complexes, five
recovered only by smina and four only by gnina, which is exactly what the Results
chapter already states.

The mechanism is not new and the Results chapter already carries it. Over the full pool,
gnina leads on validity at 87.1% against 84.5% and on near-native recovery at 176
complexes against 173, while smina leads on internal form at 237 against 235. The
composite criterion therefore flips to smina by one complex. The Discussion sentence now
follows that framing rather than inventing depth-band language, because the crossover
band is smaller than the noise the exact test can resolve.

The underlying claim, that the two refiners are not statistically separable, holds. Exact
McNemar gives p = 1.000 at both k = 1 and k = 15, on 1 and 2 discordant pairs at rank-1
and 3 and 3 at top-15.

**Lesson recorded:** both errors were the same failure, asserting a uniform ordering from
a handful of sampled depths without computing the full profile. The verification that
caught it is the one that should have run first.

## E. Collateral observation: the committed report tree was stale

Re-running the pinned `bench_pose_comparison` command changed 49 files, of which only
`topk_recovery_stats.csv` is explained by the baseline edit. Two identical consecutive
runs produced **zero** differences, so the generator is deterministic and the committed
tree simply predated the pinned flag set. No thesis float moved: all 24 shipped figure
assets stayed byte-identical and all 459 assertions still reproduce. The affected files
are diagnostics and intermediates that no float reads.
